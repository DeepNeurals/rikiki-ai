How Rikiki works

Each round everybody gets the same number of cards: 1, then 2, up to the maximum and back down. The next card of the deck is turned up and its suit is trump. When the whole deck is dealt there is no trump.

Bidding. Starting left of the dealer, each player says how many tricks they will take. The dealer bids last and may not make the bids add up to the number of tricks, so somebody has to miss.

Playing. The player left of the dealer leads. Follow the led suit if you can, otherwise play anything. The highest trump wins the trick, otherwise the highest card of the led suit. The winner leads the next trick.

Scoring. Exactly right: 5 points + 3 per trick. Wrong: −2 per trick you are off.

Coach shows the AI's estimate of your points this round for every bid or card you can play. It uses the same network as your opponents, from your seat.
